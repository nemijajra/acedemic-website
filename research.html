RESEARCH

Research Interests

Developing optical and computational technologies for biomedical applications

01

Biomedical Optics and Biophotonics

Optical imaging and spectroscopy, fluorescence imaging and spectroscopy, microscopy, optical coherence tomography (OCT), photoacoustic and optoacoustic imaging, and functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS).

02

Point-of-Care Optical Technologies

Design, development, miniaturization, and validation of compact, portable, non-invasive, and cost-effective optical devices for biomedical applications and early disease diagnosis.

03

Light–Tissue Interaction and Optical Modeling

Monte Carlo simulations, computational modeling of light propagation in biological tissues, and optimization of optical techniques for tissue characterization.

04

AI and Machine Learning for Biomedical Applications

Development of computational and machine learning methods for biomedical imaging and clinical data analysis to enable objective, rapid, and real-time diagnosis.

05

Clinical Translation and Medical Technology

Translating laboratory-based optical techniques into clinically relevant screening and diagnostic technologies through interdisciplinary collaboration with clinicians and industry.
