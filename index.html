About Me

I am a biomedical researcher at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, where I recently completed my Ph.D. in Physics. My research focuses on the interaction of light with biological tissues and the development of optical and computational technologies for biomedical applications. My work lies at the intersection of optics, photonics, biomedical engineering, and clinical medicine. I have experience in optical spectroscopy and imaging, light–tissue interaction modeling, optical instrumentation, and the design, development, and validation of portable point-of-care diagnostic devices. I also work with artificial intelligence and machine learning methods for the analysis of biomedical and clinical data to enable objective, rapid, and real-time diagnosis. A major focus of my research is translating laboratory-based optical techniques into compact, non-invasive, cost-effective, and clinically relevant technologies for disease screening and early diagnosis. I work closely with clinicians and interdisciplinary collaborators to develop and validate optical systems for real-world healthcare applications.

Research Interests

  • Biomedical Optics and Biophotonics: optical imaging and spectroscopy, fluorescence imaging and spectroscopy, microscopy, OCT, photoacoustic and optoacoustic imaging, and fNIRS.
  • Point-of-Care Optical Technologies; design, development, miniaturization, and validation of compact, portable, non-invasive, and cost-effective optical devices for biomedical applications and early disease diagnosis.
  • Light–Tissue Interaction and Optical Modeling; Monte Carlo simulations, computational modeling of light propagation in biological tissues, and optimization of optical techniques for tissue characterization.
  • AI and Machine Learning for Biomedical Applications; development of computational and machine learning methods for biomedical imaging and clinical data analysis to enable objective, rapid, and real-time diagnosis.
  • Clinical Translation and Medical Technology; translating laboratory-based optical techniques into clinically relevant screening and diagnostic technologies through interdisciplinary collaboration with clinicians and industry.
