Biomedical optics, photonics and translational medical technology

IIT Kanpur

Researcher in Biomedical Optics

I am a biomedical researcher at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, where I recently completed my Ph.D. in Physics. My research focuses on the interaction of light with biological tissues and the development of optical and computational technologies for biomedical applications.

My work lies at the intersection of optics, photonics, biomedical engineering, and clinical medicine. I have experience in optical spectroscopy and imaging, light–tissue interaction modeling, optical instrumentation, and the design, development, and validation of portable point-of-care diagnostic devices.

I also work with artificial intelligence and machine learning methods for the analysis of biomedical and clinical data to enable objective, rapid, and real-time diagnosis.

A major focus of my research is translating laboratory-based optical techniques into compact, non-invasive, cost-effective, and clinically relevant technologies for disease screening and early diagnosis. I work closely with clinicians and interdisciplinary collaborators to develop and validate optical systems for real-world healthcare applications.

I am particularly interested in advancing biomedical optics toward practical, accessible, and point-of-care technologies that can contribute to improved early disease detection and clinical decision-making.

Areas of Expertise

Optical Imaging

Fluorescence imaging, microscopy and multimodal optical imaging approaches.

Optical Instrumentation

Design, development, miniaturization and validation of biomedical optical systems.

Computational Modeling

Monte Carlo modeling of light propagation and light–tissue interaction.

Data Analysis & Signal Processing

Analysis and processing of optical and biomedical data, including signal processing, feature extraction and quantitative data analysis.

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI/ML)

Application of AI and machine learning methods to biomedical imaging, spectroscopy and clinical data for objective and automated disease diagnosis.
